SLOO Switch is a lightweight Windows app that switches between Epic Games accounts in one click, with no retyping of email and password. Accounts are saved automatically, and the interface is modern with two themes: classic wood and dark.

Features:

Instant account switching: click an account and the app closes Epic, switches, and relaunches it automatically.
Automatic account saving the moment you log in. The account stays in the app even after you log out of Epic.
A "New login" button that saves the current account, then opens Epic at the login screen.
The saved login token is refreshed every few seconds while the app is open.
Rename and delete accounts.
Automatic backup before every switch (the last 5 are kept).
Two themes: luxurious wood and modern dark.
Arabic and English interface.
A single standalone .exe, with no installer, PowerShell, or .NET needed.
It never stores passwords, and its data lives in a private folder under Program Files.
Keyboard and screen-reader friendly.
